## 💡 What It Does

This tool solves a simple problem: managing scanned (PDF) income tax and GST notices and ensuring you **never miss a deadline.**

It runs quietly in the background, stores all your data securely on your local computer, and alerts you when a due date is near.

## ✨ Features

* **Upload Any PDF Notice:** Quickly add any PDF notice (Income Tax, GST, etc.) to the dashboard.
* **Annotate & Track:** Add key details like Party Name, Notice Type, Due Date, and a Summary.
* **Automatic Pop-up Reminders:** A background app checks for deadlines every few hours and shows an interactive pop-up when a notice is due soon.
* **Set Priority:** Mark notices as "High Priority" to get alerts for them immediately.
* **Central Dashboard:** A clean web dashboard (that runs 100% locally) to see all your active and upcoming notices.
* **Completion Archive:** Mark notices as "Done" to move them to a searchable archive.
* **100% Private & Local:** All your data and PDF files are stored *only* on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud, so client data remains confidential.

## 💻 Download & Installation

The easiest way to get the app is to download the installer.

1.  Go to the **[Releases Page](https://github.com/v7309394-rgb/Tax-Notice-Dashboard/releases)**. 
    *(Assuming your repository name is "Tax-Notice-Dashboard")*
2.  Download the `TaxNoticesDashboard-Installer.exe` file from the latest release.
3.  Run the installer. It will automatically install the Dashboard and the Notifier.
4.  The Notifier will be added to your PC's startup, so you're protected automatically.

## 🚀 How to Use

1.  After installing, the **Tax Notices Dashboard** will open in your browser.
2.  Click "Choose File" to select a notice PDF and click "Upload & Annotate."
3.  Fill in the details like Party Name, Due Date, and Summary.
4.  **That's it!** The background notifier will now automatically alert you 2 days before the due date, or immediately if you marked it as "High Priority".
